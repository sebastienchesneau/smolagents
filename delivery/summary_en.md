# Meeting Summary

The kickoff meeting for the “Portail Client v2” project made it possible to define the framework for the client portal redesign over a three-month period. The current state includes significant technical debt and page-load times exceeding four seconds on the billing page, while user feedback indicates navigation difficulties in accessing invoices and a confusing complaint process. Database migration was selected as the technical priority, with an estimated duration of two weeks if the developer is fully dedicated to it, or three weeks if support duties are maintained. In parallel, the redesign of the information architecture and mock-up testing with a panel of ten clients were considered, with validation of a budget of approximately €1,500 for this panel. Integrating online payment is considered a commercial priority, with a delivery target by the end of the first month, subject to the selection and legal validation of a provider. The project will maintain a limited scope, with no unplanned new feature without the project manager's approval. Weekly follow-up meetings are scheduled every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m.
