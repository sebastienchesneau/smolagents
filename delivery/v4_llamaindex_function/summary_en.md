# Meeting Summary

The kick-off meeting for the “Portail Client v2” project established the framework for the customer portal redesign, to be completed over three months. The team identified technical debt and page load times exceeding four seconds on the billing page as priority issues, and decided to prioritize the database migration. This migration is estimated to take two weeks if Marc is dedicated to it, or three weeks if he handles support in parallel; he will therefore be relieved of support duties during this period. In parallel, Sofia will conduct mock-up testing with a panel of ten customers, with an approved budget of approximately €1,500, to redesign the information architecture and the invoice- and claim-related journeys. Online payment integration was retained as a commercial priority, with a delivery target by the end of the first month, subject to selecting and obtaining legal approval for a provider. A weekly follow-up is scheduled every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m., and the scope must remain limited to planned features unless Claire approves otherwise.
