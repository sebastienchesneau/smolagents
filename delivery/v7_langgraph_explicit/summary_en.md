# Meeting Summary

The kick-off meeting for the "Portail Client v2" project defined the scope for the client portal redesign over a three-month period. The current portal has significant technical debt and page load times exceeding four seconds on the billing page, while users have navigation difficulties, particularly in finding their invoices and submitting a complaint. The team decided to prioritize the database migration, estimated at two weeks if Marc is dedicated to it, with support duties removed during that period. In parallel, Sofia will conduct mockup testing with a panel of ten clients, for which a budget of approximately €1,500 was approved. Integrating online payment was identified as a commercial priority, with a target delivery by the end of the first month, subject to the selection and legal validation of a provider. The scope will remain strictly controlled, with no unplanned new feature without Claire's approval, and weekly follow-up meetings are scheduled every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m.
