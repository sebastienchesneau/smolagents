Julien emphasized the commercial importance of online payment and the objective of delivering it by the end of the first month, while the choice of provider will need to include legal approval. The project will maintain a tightly defined scope, with no unplanned feature additions without Claire’s approval, and will be monitored through weekly check-ins every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m.
