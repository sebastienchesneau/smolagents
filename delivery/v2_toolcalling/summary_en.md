# Meeting Summary

The launch meeting for the "Portail Client v2" project, planned over three months, established the framework for redesigning the client portal. Marc highlighted the technical debt of the current portal and loading times exceeding four seconds on the billing page, leading to the database migration being prioritized. This migration is estimated to take two weeks if Marc is fully dedicated to it, or three weeks if he also provides support, and Claire agreed in principle to relieve him of support during that period. Sofia noted navigation difficulties, particularly in finding invoices and submitting a complaint, and the redesign of the information architecture will be supported by tests with ten clients, with a budget of €1,500 approved. Julien identified online payment integration as one of the commercial priorities, with a goal of delivery by the end of the first month, subject to selecting a provider and obtaining legal approval. The project will be monitored in a weekly meeting on Tuesdays at 10:00 a.m., and its scope will remain limited to the planned features unless Claire approves otherwise.
