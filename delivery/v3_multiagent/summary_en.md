# Meeting Summary

The kick-off meeting for the three-month “Client Portal v2” project established the scope for the client portal redesign. The team identified significant technical debt in the current portal, whose billing page takes more than four seconds to load, as well as navigation difficulties and a confusing complaint process for clients. Database migration was selected as a priority, with Marc estimating that it would require two weeks if he is fully dedicated to it, or three weeks if he retains support duties; Claire agreed that he would be relieved of support during that period. In parallel, Sofia will begin redesigning the information architecture and conducting mockup testing with a panel of ten clients, for which Claire approved a budget of approximately €1,500. Julien emphasized the commercial importance of online payment and his objective of delivering it by the end of the first month, while Claire requested a comparison of three providers for the following week and plans to involve the legal department in their validation. Governance will be based on a weekly meeting every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m., a detailed migration schedule expected from Marc before Friday, and a strict scope prohibiting any unplanned feature without Claire's approval.
