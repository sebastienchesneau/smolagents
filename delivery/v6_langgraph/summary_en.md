# Meeting Summary

The kick-off meeting for the "Portail Client v2" project defined the scope for redesigning the customer portal over a three-month period. The current system has significant technical debt and page load times exceeding four seconds on the billing page, while users have navigation difficulties, notably finding their invoices and submitting a complaint. Migration of the database is prioritized ahead of the other work; it is estimated at two weeks if Marc is dedicated to it, or three weeks if he handles support in parallel, and it was decided to relieve him of support during this period. In parallel, the information architecture must be redesigned and the mock-ups tested with a panel of ten customers, with an approved budget of approximately €1,500. Integrating online payment is identified as a commercial priority, with the objective of delivering it by the end of the first month, subject to selecting a provider and obtaining legal approval. A weekly check-in is set for every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m., and the scope must remain strictly limited to the planned features without Claire's approval.
