# Meeting Summary

The kick-off meeting for the "Portail Client v2" project made it possible to frame the redesign of the client portal, planned over three months. Marc highlighted the technical debt of the current portal and loading times exceeding four seconds on the billing page, while Sofia noted navigation difficulties, particularly in finding invoices and submitting a complaint. The team decided in favor of migrating the database before the other developments, estimated at two weeks if Marc is fully dedicated to it, or three weeks if he handles support in parallel. The budget of approximately €1,500 for a panel of ten clients to test mock-ups was approved. The integration of online payment is identified as a commercial priority, with a delivery target at the end of the first month, subject to the selection and legal validation of a provider. The project will be monitored during weekly meetings on Tuesdays at 10:00 a.m., and its scope will remain limited to features approved by Claire.
