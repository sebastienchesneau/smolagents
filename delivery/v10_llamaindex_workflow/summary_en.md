# Meeting Summary

The kick-off meeting for the “Portail Client v2” project aimed to define the scope of the client portal redesign over a three-month period. The main findings concern technical debt related to an outdated framework, loading times exceeding four seconds on the billing page, as well as navigation difficulties and a confusing complaint process. Database migration was selected as the initial priority, with an estimated duration of two weeks if Marc is relieved of support duties, while Sofia will simultaneously conduct mockup testing with a panel of ten clients. A budget of approximately €1,500 was approved for recruiting this panel. Julien emphasized the commercial importance of online payment, which is desired before the end of the first month, but its integration remains contingent on selecting a provider and obtaining approval from the legal department. The project will maintain a strict scope, with any unplanned feature requiring Claire’s prior approval, and weekly meetings will take place every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m.
